Remove TIGA

Uninstall stops TIGA services and removes desktop shortcuts. Your indexed data in tiga_work/ is kept unless you choose to delete it in the uninstaller.

On this PC, double-click the desktop shortcut Uninstall TIGA, or run uninstall.bat / uninstall.sh from the TIGA install folder.
